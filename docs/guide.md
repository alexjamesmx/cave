# Using the Cave

What each part of the app does. How it is built and run: `docs/system.md`.

## The shape

- **Unlock once per device** with the server token; without it the app shows only a lock screen. Settings → Devices & server → Lock this device, or Forget this device to also delete its copy.
- **Six sections** on the rail at the left (icons; the name shows on hover, or always with **Show names** at the bottom of the rail, remembered on this device): Money, Calendar, Flows, Projects, Notes, Tasks, and Settings at the bottom. On a phone the rail is a dock at the bottom: four sections you choose (More → Edit dock, or Settings → General), Ask in the middle, More for the rest.
- **New from anywhere:** the rail's + (⌘K or Ctrl+K, the dock's middle button on a phone) opens one sheet of short forms: Money, Task, Reminder, Subscription, Note. **N** opens it on a note. A save closes the sheet and shows what was saved for a moment. There is no chat in the app: agents read and write the Cave through the connector.
- **The app opens on the Scene** (Projects → Scene): you in the middle, every active project an orb around you. Size is activity this week, colour is status, a dashed ring means an agent is on it; paused projects sit on the outer ring, dim. Hover shows the agent's latest report; click enters the project. The same orbs are a list for the keyboard and screen readers. Above it, **Needs you**: questions agents asked, blocked parts and flows waiting for approval, each with its actions; it hides when there is nothing. What is due is Calendar's.

## Projects

- **Scene**, **All** (cards: a status dot, "72% · on track · agent working", the next due part; Needs you on top), **Ideas**, **Activity** (agents' runs, filtered by status; flows' and jobs' runs are under Flows → Runs, a source filter away here), and a tab per pinned project.
- **A project's status** is computed, never typed: done, dropped or paused from its own status; **blocked** when a part is blocked; **waiting on you** when an agent asked a question; **slipping** when its review date passed, a part is overdue, or progress is more than 15 % behind the schedule between its creation and its latest part due date; else **on track**. Progress is the template's rule (LeetCode: problems solved), else parts done, else tasks done.
- **Every project has the same page:** a status ring, the goal and a meta line (where it lives, agents in the last 30 days, parts done, the last report, the next review), then
  - **Overview:** the Plan (parts as a checklist in order, the current one marked, a blocked one with its reason), the latest from the agent (its last step, and its open question with Answer), Connected (where it lives, notes, flows, and every collection with items of the project), open tasks, Add task, Ask about this project.
  - **Board:** the project's tasks as a kanban.
  - **Pages:** the project's documents in Markdown (resources, the plan, lists): a list with a colour dot and who wrote each, New page, and one page open in the editor (a toolbar for headings, bold, italic, lists, checklists, quotes, code and links, a live preview beside the text, saved on blur and after two seconds idle, `[[Title]]` linking the project's own pages first); Rename, Colour and Delete, which keeps the page in the history. If a page changed elsewhere while you were typing, the editor asks before writing over it.
  - **Problems** (a learning project such as LeetCode): the problems tracked on it, with solved by difficulty, this week and what is due for review on top, chips by status, Group by list, topic, pattern, difficulty or status, and a quick form (number, title, difficulty, list, URL); the drawer has the rest (attempts, minutes, review date, notes).
  - **Diagram:** Mermaid text (flowcharts, sequence, class, state, ER) rendered beside the editor; saved when you leave the box; a mistake shows under the editor and the last good drawing stays. Agents edit it as the `diagram` field.
  - **Canvas:** a whiteboard with the parts, tasks and items as cards; drag to arrange, add sticky notes, frames and shapes (box, rounded box, diamond) with text, connect parts and shapes for "depends on"; view-only on a phone. Active runs glow beside their part.
  - **Activity:** the project's runs, filtered by agent.
- **Ideas:** yours, newest first: capture one, promote it to a project with a template (Generic, Software, Learning with a problem tracker, Study with pages and parts, Trip, Goal), or start one with Claude. Agents' finds left this page in M12; the `radar` collection stays under Settings → Data.
- **Agents ask you** through tasks: a question from `log_step` becomes "Answer: …" in the project, listed under Needs you and on the project's Overview; write the answer in the task's body and mark it done, and the agent reads it back with `get_project`.

## Money

- **Add** an expense or income from Transactions (New entry: the form stays open for the next receipt) or the New sheet, for any day and time (Today, Yesterday). Pick Expense or Income first: the category is a searchable select that offers only that direction's categories, here and when you edit an entry; transfers (investments) count as neither spending nor income. With one account in the list it is taken without asking; add another under Categories & accounts and the account picker returns.
- **Transactions:** one month at a time (arrows, or All time) with its in, out and net on top; chips All, Expenses, Income, Transfers and Uncategorized (no category, or one that is no longer in the list); search; Group by day, category or account, each group with its subtotal, collapsible. Undated entries are listed and counted apart, never filled in.
- **Overview:** month or year. Spent, Income, Saved and Invested (transfers into Investment) with their change against the previous period and a 12-month line; the month day by day; where it went against last period, each row opening the ledger on that category; the last 12 months stacked by category; top titles and the largest entries. Filters are kept in the URL.
- **Budget:** two amounts you type (Overview → Budget → Edit, or Settings → General): a monthly limit and a saving target. The Budget tile shows what is left (or how far over) and where the month ends at the current pace; the year uses twelve times the limit. Crossing 80 % and 100 % of the limit notifies you once each a month, on every channel that is ready. Claude reads the same numbers through `money_month`.
- **Subscriptions:** one table of what you pay for every month or year: name, amount, how often, its cost a month, the next charge (it moves on by itself), since when and how much you've spent. On top, what the active ones cost a month and a year, how many are active, and everything spent. Spent and Since come from your real expenses: under the Subscriptions category with the same name, or linked to it from the expense's Payment field, so a month it was paused simply has no charge. Chips show Active, Paused or All; a row opens it to edit, **Pause** or **Resume**, or Delete. It never reminds on its own: for a reminder, make a flow that starts **When a date arrives** on Payments' due date.
- **Net worth:** what you own minus what you owe.
- **Categories & accounts:** the two lists every expense, income and payment picks from (name, emoji, colour, kind, order, archive). Other lists (task statuses, idea stages) are edited in the field editor under Settings → Data.

## Calendar and reminders

- **Reminder:** one form everywhere (New, a day in Month, Upcoming, the Reminders table): Title, Date, Time (empty for the whole day) and Repeat. Open a reminder to change it, mark it Done or Delete it.
- **Done and Snooze** on every reminder (the form, the table, Upcoming, Needs you), as two small buttons beside it. A repeating reminder moves to its next occurrence and stays open; the 31st becomes the 30th in shorter months and returns to the 31st. Undo puts the previous values back.
- **Month** (or `C`): a grid with repeats shown ahead; click a day's number to add a reminder on it, a reminder to edit it. Beside it, **Upcoming**: overdue on top, then today and the next 14 days from every source, with + for a new reminder. **Reminders**: a table like Transactions: Upcoming, Overdue, Done and All with counts, search, a repeat filter, grouped by day, the repeat beside each title, Done and Snooze on each row. **Routine**: the week you aim for, a guide and nothing more: one column per weekday, blocks coloured by their area (Body, Learn, Rest, Fun, Other), the "some times a week" ones under Any day, and each area's hours a week on top. A block is on set days with a start, every day, or some times a week; a day's + adds one on that day, a click edits it. The routine has no dates, never notifies and counts nothing. The old Agenda and Week paths open Month and Routine. Scheduled flows show their next run.
- A reminder with a date and no time is due at the reminder time in Settings → General (09:00 by default).

## Flows

- **Flows:** one canvas with every flow, grouped by what starts it (On a schedule, each collection it watches, Needs fixing), and the **System** jobs that keep the Cave running (scheduled flows, Telegram, backups, the off-box copy, the runs trim) as a read-only group of their own. Everything the Cave tells you runs through flows, and five come installed: **Reminder notifications** (a reminder when it is due, with Done and Snooze), **Bill notifications** (a payment that isn't a subscription, the day before), **Budget at 80%** and **Budget at 100%** (once a month), and the **Daily digest** (one email a day, off until you turn it on; its time is its schedule). Edit, turn off or delete them like any flow. Each flow is a lane: its card (name, On, Off, Draft or Needs approval, its last run) then its trigger and steps, "else stop" under an if; a flow that is on glows and its edges move. Click a card or a node to open it in the inspector beside the canvas (`/flows/<id>`), where the forms edit it; they come from the same registry that validates flows and gives agents their schema, and edits show on the canvas until you Save. Text can use `{{item.amount|pct:20}}`, `{{today}}` and the like from a picker. **New flow** sits on the canvas; Duplicate copies one as a draft; Delete removes one (it stops running and stays in the history). A built-in job opens read-only with what it does, its schedule and its last and next run.
- A trigger is a schedule, an item added, a field changed, **a date arriving** (any date field of a collection, optionally some days before; a repeating date arrives on each date of its series, a paused or done item is skipped, and one seen more than 6 hours late is let go), or **spending reaching** a share of the monthly limit; steps are if, create an item, change the trigger item, notify (opening the trigger item, it carries what that item needs: Done and Snooze on a reminder), send an email (to you, or to up to 5 addresses, with its own subject, message and sender name), and send the daily digest. **Test run** shows what each step would do and writes and sends nothing. Turn on when it is right.
- **Runs:** everything the Cave ran on its own, newest first: each flow run with its steps' outcomes (anything it wrote links to the item and has Undo) and each System job's run with its result; filter to flows or System. A flow's own page keeps its run history too. Flows waiting for approval show on the canvas, under Needs you and in Settings → Agents. Agents' runs are under Projects → Activity.
- **Two drafts to start from:** "Rent reminder" and "Salary → save". **Agents build flows** through `save_flow`; one that changes existing items waits for your Approve (Flows, Settings → Agents, Needs you, or Telegram if you allow it); see `docs/agents.md`.

## Notes

- **Notes** (`/notes/all`): everything you write that is not a project's page, as a gallery grouped by topic (A to Z, "No topic" last, newest first in each), or a table with **Table** (`?view=table`). Search covers title, topic and text. **New note** opens a blank one: a title, a topic (a `/`-separated path such as `School/Calculus`), a colour, and Markdown in the same editor as pages and the diary; a ```` ```mermaid ```` block draws as a diagram, `[[Title]]` links another note. Delete keeps it in the history. The Ask sheet's Note form saves here unless you pick a project; agents' `add_note` without a project lands here under the topic Inbox. A page whose project is deleted comes back here.
- **Today** (`/notes/write`): one day full width: the entry in the Markdown editor (the same toolbar, preview and autosave as pages; **Preview** shows or hides the rendered text beside it, remembered on this device), the habits as toggles, a colour and Delete entry (kept in the history). Arrows move between days, and the day follows today until you pick one, so a diary left open past midnight moves on.
- **Days:** the month as a grid: a dot for a written day, a strip of the habits logged, the day's colour as its tint, today outlined; beside it the habits' last 30 days and streaks, and **Add habit** adds one (it becomes a toggle on every day). Click a day to read it (`/notes/days?day=`): the entry rendered as a page, the habits done under it, arrows to the day before and after. **Edit** opens the same editor as Today; **Read** goes back. The old Habits and Months pages open Days, and every `/diary` link opens its tab here.

## Tasks

- **Board** for the work in flight: To do, Doing, Waiting; **Show done** adds Done and Dropped back (remembered on this device). **List**: a table with Open, Done, Dropped and All, search, open tasks by due date. Tasks with a due date are in Calendar → Month too. One New task button; drag between columns (press and hold on a phone), arrow keys move a focused card. A task with a project carries a bar in the Projects colour. A repeating task's Done brings the same task back on its next date. Questions from agents are tasks too, until answered.

## Settings

- **General:** your name and goal (the Scene's centre), time zone, reminder time, theme, what the app opens on, motion (on, or follow the system's reduced-motion setting), the phone dock.
- **Devices & server:** this device (lock, forget), the devices that synced in the last 30 days, the server (version, last deploy, backups, off-box copy), integrations; sub-sections for Schedules (the next 7 days: reminders, flow slots, jobs, the deploy check), Jobs (and their runs) and Notifications (the log by channel and key, titles never stored; the Dev outbox outside production).
- **Email:** Settings → Notifications → Email sets who every email goes to and the name it comes from (the address stays the server's verified one); a flow's Send an email step can name its own.
- **Notifications:** Web Push on this device (on iPhone, from the Home Screen app), and the channels: Telegram once linked, Discord with a webhook, Email through Resend (`docs/hosting.md` step 12). Web Push is private and the default; Telegram and Discord get titles only unless you choose full detail; email goes to your own inbox, so it gets full detail. What notifies is a flow (see Flows): this page only says where notifications go and how much each channel is told.
- **Agents:** flows waiting for approval, delete proposals, and each agent token with its permissions and use. Agents' runs are under Projects → Activity.
- **Data:** Download export (every record as JSON; `bun run export` does the same on the server), Import (an export file back in: compared with this device first, then only what differs is written, as edits from here, so it syncs and can be undone; nothing is removed), every collection raw with its views and the field editor, and the undo history of agents' writes.

## Notifications

- **Reminders and bills reach you when they're due**, within a minute, on every channel that is ready, through their flows; the budget flows tell you at 80 % and 100 % of the limit. **Act from the notification:** Done and Snooze 1h open the Cave and apply there (in an email they are links); in Telegram the buttons work in the chat, and `/remind`, `/due` and `/help` work too.
- Setup: `docs/hosting.md` step 12.

## Backups

- **Automatic.** The server takes a backup when it starts, if the newest is more than a day old, and checks again every hour.
  - Each backup is a full SQLite copy, checked with `integrity_check` before it counts. A copy that fails is renamed `*.failed` and kept.
  - It keeps the newest 14, plus the newest of each of the last 12 months, and deletes only older backup files.
- **Where.** `data/backups/`, or the folder in `BACKUP_DIR`. On the VPS: `/var/lib/cave/backups`, copied off-box to R2 (`docs/system.md` section 4).
  - File names are UTC times: `cave-2026-10-13T1405.db`.
  - **Backups hold everything, every amount and note, unencrypted. Keep them in a private place.**
- **By hand.** `bun run backup` takes one now, even while the server runs.
- **Restore.** Stop the server, then `bun run restore <backup file>`.
  - Restore is for a lost or broken server. It does not roll data back: devices push everything newer than the backup straight back. To reverse a change, use Undo under Settings → Data (agents' writes) or set the value back from the item's history in its drawer.
  - The current database is renamed `cave.db.before-restore-<time>`, never deleted.
  - The restored database gets a new id. On their next sync, devices push back anything newer than the backup.
